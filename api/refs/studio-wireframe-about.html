About

Independent design
studio out of the UK.

Hi, I'm Ethan — a creator working in Bristol, England. My work spans across several disciplines including brand design, digital products and all the craft details in between.

For over 20 years, I've been captivated by design. It started in the early 2000s, building custom interfaces (or “skins”) for members on creative boards like PixelPoint.

Across these years, I've had the fortune to collaborate with remarkable brands and alongside genuinely brilliant teammates. Every build has challenged me to become a sharper thinker, to elevate our quality, and to continue honing my practice. I'm grateful for what Gridline built, but far more energized by what lies ahead.
