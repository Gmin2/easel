An automated yield engine powering Harbor Markets.

Harbor v3 delivers modular lending engines across Ethereum and EVM systems, offering native architecture to embed lending and borrowing with automated risk rails and SDKs powered by smart contracts and HarborKit.

Developers can use LendKit React, LendKit TypeScript, orLendKit API to connect base protocoloperations and data across wallets, aggregators, institutional desks, and Web3 applications.

Supply

Supplying assets to Harbor lets users earn interest and, optionally, use supplied tokens as collateral for borrowing. When an asset is supplied, aTokens (e.g., aUSDC, aWETH) are minted as interest-bearing ERC-20 tokens whose balance increases over time from borrowing activity in the pool.

Redeeming exchanges hTokens back to base assets, including accumulated interest, determined by unutilized pool reserves and the collateral health of any associated active borrow positions.

Borrow

Borrowing allows builders to unlock capital against deposited protocol reserves. Every loan stays over-collateralized, ensuring locked collateral value always covers open debt. Market risk is monitored via a Health Factorand asset-level liquidation triggers; once the Health Metric breaches safe limits, collateral is eligible for liquidation. When borrowers draw funds, debt is tracked via variableDebtTokens (e.g., variableDebtUSDC), ERC-20 tokens reflecting cumulative borrowed debt and accrued financing interest.

Debtors may settle obligations anytime, with loans remaining active while collateral ratios stay healthy. The Health Metric updates against dynamic collateral valuations (oracle feeds and compounding rates). Whenever it drops below 1, the vault faces liquidation where outside bots can close outstanding debt to claim discounted collateral.

Interest Rates

Interest rates adjust with utilization. v3 uses an interest rate model with an optimal utilization point: below the optimal point, borrow rates rise gradually; above it, they rise faster with the second slope.

Lending yields derive directly from borrower interest minus a protocol reserve cut directed to the Harbor treasury.

Liquidations

If a loan's Health Metric declines below 1, third parties may trigger liquidation. A keeper settles partial debt to acquire underlying collateral at a discount (the incentive bonus). Risk thresholds and bonuses are parameterized per market and readable via onchain interfaces.

Harbor Earn Vaults provide ERC-4626 compatible yield primitives allowing Harbor deposit balances to be automated with modular fee logic and manager controls. Upon depositing eligible tokens, users obtain vault tokens reflecting their proportional interest across underlying reserves and generated yields. Positions can be exited for base tokens plus accumulated gains based on pool liquidity, allowing passive capital expansion.

To strategy curators, Harbor Earn Vaults deliver a programmable framework for executing lending automation while collecting management rewards on performance. Full ERC-4626 alignment ensures turnkey integration into diverse DeFi architectures and apps, simplifying deployment across aggregators or nested protocols. Each vault inherits the rigorous security standards and deep capital pools established by Harbor Markets.

Harbor v3 Core Engine

Harbor v3 brings powerful architectural upgrades across earlier deployments, designed to optimize balance sheet velocity, insulate against risk, and expand Harbor across more than 14+ multi-chain ecosystems.

Efficiency Mode (E-Mode)

Efficiency Mode unlocks peak capital utilization for pegged assets. Whenever accounts deposit and borrow within identical E-Mode groupings (such as USD-backed stablecoins), they access boosted loan-to-value (LTV) limits. This unlocks minimal-slippage, leveraged executions including basis trades on staked ETH assets or native currency swaps.

Isolation Mode

Isolation Mode guarantees safe onboarding of exotic or volatile collateral without exposing wider pool liquidity to contagion. Assets marked under Isolation Mode serve as collateral exclusively for borrowing designated stable assets, bounded by an explicit borrowing cap. This insulates systemic stability while broadening protocol token support.

Inspect debt parameters and isolation borrow limits directly in theparameter dashboard.

Siloed Borrowing

Siloed borrowing acts as a market constraint restricting borrowers of a specific asset to that single liability, blocking them from opening concurrent borrows of other assets in the pool.

Check borrow limits and siloed market rules on theparameter dashboard.
